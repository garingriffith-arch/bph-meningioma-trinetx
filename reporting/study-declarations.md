# Study declarations

- **Study design:** Active-comparator, new-user EHR cohort, with an outcome-free landmark primary analysis and separate observational day-zero sensitivity analyses.
- **Ethics:** Institutional Research Feasibility Determination STUDY00030063 (April 7, 2026) classified the study as non-human-subjects research; IRB oversight was not required.
- **Protocol:** No separate written prospective study protocol was prepared. Exploratory analyses should not be characterized as prospectively prespecified.
- **Funding:** None reported.
- **Competing interests:** None reported.
- **Other disclosures:** None reported.
- **Data availability:** TriNetX EHR source data are controlled-access and are not distributed through this repository.
- **Code availability:** Analysis source code is publicly accessible here without an open-source license. Data-use and institutional disclosure restrictions continue to apply.
- **Data source interpretation:** The `Ex-US` source category refers to healthcare organizations outside the United States, not necessarily patient residence. Medication records may reflect prescriptions or administrations; their presence does not establish dispensing or adherence.

Author names, affiliations and complete journal-specific declarations should be taken from the submitted manuscript, not inferred from this code repository.
